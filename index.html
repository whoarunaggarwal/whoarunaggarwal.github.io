Professional Identity

Pioneering Industrial Control & Team Leadership

Bridging the gap between embedded hardware, real-time control software, and high-throughput automated manufacturing.

With approximately 8 years of progressive experience, I lead control software engineering teams delivering mission-critical industrial automation systems for premier technology and automotive manufacturers worldwide. Currently, as Technical Lead (Manager) – Control Software at VVDN Technologies, I lead a dedicated team of 8 control software engineers.

"Industrial automation software is not merely about writing code; it is about guaranteeing deterministic sub-millisecond safety, zero-defect quality control, and uninterrupted 24/7 manufacturing uptime."

My responsibilities span high-availability system architecture, cross-functional foresight studies, task delegation, and direct technical mentorship.
